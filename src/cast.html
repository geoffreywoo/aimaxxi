ARCHIVE EXERCISE · FICTION

YOUR OLD ROOM IS READY.

Keep the move. Change the next choice.

The Temp wants to keep reading in room 101. I insist on checkout at eleven. At her request, the robot moves her occupied chair to room 102. Every room is ready; the machine does its job.

Then I tell her: “Your old room is ready.”

In the original, she says “I’ll take it” and returns to room 101.

Replace that line and the last panel. Give the Temp a different choice. Show what it makes me do.

Keep the robot competent and rooms 101 and 102 separate.

One alternate line and a drawn closing panel, or a text description of the closing action, is enough. Label your adaptation community-made fiction.
