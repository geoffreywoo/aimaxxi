HOUSE
SITTING.

A small favor. A change of scenery.

The scene, in text

At the blue house with an orange door and round attic window, I leave my key with a robot friend.

CEO: Can you house-sit?
ROBOT: Of course.

I’m already at the beach. Back at the house, the robot packs a sunhat beside a watered plant.

The same intact house arrives on a wheeled carrier at that shore. The robot sits on its porch in the sunhat; I stay in my beach chair. We each raise a cold drink toward the other. The house is looked after. We both have our holiday.
